FAQ

Working with Arche.

Anything unanswered? Ask us directly — we're happy to talk it through.

01
What does Arche do?

We build digital systems. Today that means four things: video editing, websites, AI chat agents and automation. You can hire us for one. Over time they can connect.

02
Which services are currently available?

Exactly four: video editing, web development, AI chatbots and AI automation. That's the current offering — focused so each one ships to a high standard.

03
Can I hire Arche for only one service?

Yes. Most engagements start with a single service. You don't need everything at once — start where the friction is.

04
Who do you work with?

Small and growing teams who feel the cost of disconnected tools. We're a studio, not a consultancy with a hundred people — which is why the work stays close to the people who design it.

05
Can you work with our existing tools?

That's the point of Connect. We extend the CRMs, inboxes, sheets and APIs you already run rather than replacing them.

06
How is pricing determined?

Some work has a defined package. Custom work is quoted after a short conversation — scope, timeline and investment in writing before anything starts.

07
How long does a project take?

Edits ship in days. Landing pages in about two weeks. Sites, automations and agents typically two to eight weeks depending on scope. Every quote includes a timeline.

08
What happens after I submit an inquiry?

You'll get a reply within one working day. For packages we confirm scope and schedule. For custom work we book a short call, then send a written proposal.

09
Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every build includes a support window. Most clients continue with a light maintenance or iteration arrangement afterwards — that's the Evolve step.
